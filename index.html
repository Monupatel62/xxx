How to Play Catch the Coin

Catch the Coin is a free browser arcade game. Move a basket left and right to catch falling gold coins before they hit the ground. Each caught coin scores points — miss 3 coins and the game ends. Survive a 60-second countdown across 3 escalating difficulty stages and beat your personal high score.

Desktop controls: Use the ← → arrow keys or A / D to move the basket. Press ESC or P to pause. Press M to mute sound.

Mobile controls: Tap anywhere on screen to move the basket toward that position, or use the on-screen D-pad buttons in the bottom-left corner.

Tips to Beat Your High Score

The key to a top score is building and maintaining the combo multiplier. Catch coins consecutively without missing to reach ×4 multiplier — at that level, a single gold coin gives 40 points instead of 10. Prioritize catching every coin over going for bonus stars if it risks breaking your combo. In Stage 3, keep the basket near center so you can reach either side quickly as spawn rate increases.
