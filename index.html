Catch the Coin — Free Online Arcade Game

Play Catch the Coin free online — a fast-paced browser arcade game where you move a basket left and right to catch falling gold coins before they hit the ground. No download, no install, no login required. Works on desktop and mobile.

How to Play Catch the Coin

Use the left and right arrow keys to move your basket. On mobile, tap the screen to move toward that position. Catch falling gold coins to score points — each coin is worth 10 points. Miss 3 coins and it's game over.
